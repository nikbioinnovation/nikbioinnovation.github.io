Who we are

Application science, applied to your lab's daily reality.

NIK BIO-INNOVATION was founded to close the gap between what a diagnostic instrument can do and what a laboratory actually gets out of it day to day. We work across molecular diagnostics, immunoassay platforms, and transfusion medicine — helping labs validate methods, resolve complaints, pass audits, and train staff with the same rigor used in manufacturer-side quality systems.

◆

Complaint investigation, root-cause analysis, and QA documentation built the way IVD manufacturers expect it — not generic advice.

◆

Multi-platform fluency

Field-tested in Gujarat
