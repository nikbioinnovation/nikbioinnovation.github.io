About us

Built on years at the bench, not just behind a desk.

NIK BIO-INNOVATION exists because diagnostic laboratories need advisors who have actually run the assays, faced the audits, and fielded the complaints.

Company profile

A consultancy grounded in field application experience

NIK BIO-INNOVATION is an Ahmedabad-based diagnostics consultancy providing technical, application, and quality-systems support to hospitals, blood banks, and diagnostic laboratories across Gujarat. Our work spans molecular diagnostics, ELISA and CLIA immunoassay platforms, and transfusion medicine — covering everything from instrument troubleshooting to full NABL accreditation documentation.

We work alongside laboratory teams rather than simply issuing recommendations from outside — investigating field complaints, validating methods against reference platforms, and preparing the exact documentation an assessor will expect to see.

BaseAhmedabad, Gujarat
FocusMolecular · ELISA · CLIA · Blood Bank
FrameworkNABL / ISO 15189
ModeField visits & remote support
Mission

Close the gap between instrument and outcome.

To give diagnostic laboratories the same depth of technical, quality, and application support that manufacturers reserve for their own field teams — so every assay result a lab reports is one it can stand behind.

Vision

A trusted technical partner for every lab in Gujarat.

To become the first call for laboratories across Gujarat facing a complaint, a validation challenge, an audit, or a new platform decision — known for rigor, responsiveness, and vendor-neutral advice.
